Frequently asked questions

My photos still look blurry. Why?

A fast shutter needs light. In bright conditions ApexShot freezes motion easily; in dim light it keeps the shutter fast and lets the image get a little darker or noisier, because motion blur can’t be recovered. Try Very Fast subject class in good light, and make sure the subject is inside the focus reticle.

Where are my shots saved?

The sharpest frame of each capture is saved automatically to an album called “ApexShot” in your Photos library. Open the thumbnail in the viewfinder to browse, zoom, share or delete them.

What are the three modes?

  • Action — the default: keeps a fast shutter for moving subjects.
  • Panning — helps you follow the subject for a sharp subject and streaked background.
  • Trap Focus — lock on a spot and the subject triggers the burst as it crosses.

Which iPhones are supported?

ApexShot requires iOS 26 and runs on iPhone. It uses every available lens (ultra-wide, wide, telephoto) and adapts to your device’s capabilities.

Does ApexShot use my data?

No. There is no tracking, no analytics and no account. Everything stays on your device. See the privacy policy.
